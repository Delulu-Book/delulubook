The Mirror — Companion App

Technology doesn't dissolve delusion — it magnifies it. The Mirror uses the same toolkit for reflection: when you can see what's going on inside, it's easier to help yourself.

We don't lack intention. We lack alignment. The Mirror measures the gap between what you intend to do and what actually happens — not what you say or plan, but what you do. If you want, it can add physiological and behavioral context (heart rate, HRV, hesitation patterns). It doesn't diagnose, reward, or gamify you — it shows you what happened.

You don't beat delusion with ideas. You beat it with data.
