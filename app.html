The Mirror

30-Day Delulu Check

Launching March 26, 2026

We all tell ourselves stories.
Some good. Some bad.
The Mirror doesn’t care which.

The app doesn’t tell you what to believe. It shows you what happens when you confront what you already believe.

The Mirror reads your biosignals — heart rate, heart rate variability, electrodermal activity — alongside your behavioral data. A multi-fusion ML model processes it all to understand your internal state in real time.

For 30 days, it tracks that state. How you react to conversations, stress, attachment, ego, fear. And how — with consistency — your nervous system adapts.

You’ll see what triggers you. What destabilizes you. What owns you. And what doesn’t.

You don’t beat delusion with ideas. You beat it with data.
